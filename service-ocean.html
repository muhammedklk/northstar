WHY OCEAN FREIGHT

The world's cargo,
in good hands.

Ocean freight remains the most cost-effective way to move large volumes of goods across the globe. Northstar's sea freight team manages every aspect — from booking and documentation through to port handling and final delivery — with end-to-end container visibility.

Whether you need a full container load (FCL), a shared groupage shipment (LCL), or a complex multi-port supply route, our team structures the right solution around your timeline and budget.

WHAT WE OFFER

Our ocean freight services.

🚢

Full Container Load (FCL)

Dedicated container for your cargo only — ideal for large volumes, fragile goods, or shipments requiring secure, sealed transport.

📦

Less than Container (LCL)

Share container space with other shippers — a cost-efficient solution for smaller volumes without long waiting times.

⚓

Project & OOG Cargo

Out-of-gauge and project cargo solutions for machinery, industrial equipment, and oversized freight requiring special handling.

🌍

Multi-Port Routing

Complex supply chain routing across multiple countries and ports — structured to minimise transit time and customs delays.

📋

Trade Documentation

Bill of lading, certificates of origin, insurance, and all export/import paperwork handled by our experienced documentation team.

🔒

Reefer & Hazmat

Refrigerated containers for perishables and certified dangerous goods handling for chemicals, gases, and classified cargo.
