WHY ROAD & RAIL

The backbone of
every supply chain.

Ground freight is the connective tissue of global logistics. From the first mile out of a factory to the last mile into a distribution centre, Northstar's road and rail network keeps your cargo moving efficiently across borders and regions.

Our intermodal solutions combine the flexibility of road with the cost and scale advantages of rail — giving you the best of both, managed as one seamless service.

WHAT WE OFFER

Our road & rail services.

🚛

Full Truckload (FTL)

Dedicated truck for your cargo — fastest road option with no shared stops, direct routing from origin to destination.

📦

Less-than-Truckload (LTL)

Share truck space with other shippers and only pay for what you use — ideal for smaller, regular freight consignments.

🚂

Rail Freight

Cost-effective and eco-friendly rail solutions for heavy, bulky, or high-volume cargo on established rail corridors.

🔄

Intermodal Solutions

Seamless road-to-rail or rail-to-road transfers managed end-to-end — combining the advantages of both modes.

📍

First & Last Mile

Dedicated collection and delivery from factory floor to final customer — connecting your ocean or air shipments to ground.

🌍

Cross-Border Freight

Expert handling of cross-border documentation, customs, and permits for multi-country ground transport routes.
